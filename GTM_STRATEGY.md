# JobMatch GTM Strategy
*Go-To-Market Plan for Career Assessment Platform*

---

## Executive Summary

JobMatch is a psychology-first career matching platform that helps users discover tech careers suited to their cognitive style. This GTM strategy outlines how to acquire users, convert them to paying customers, and build sustainable revenue.

---

## Target Market

### Primary Segments

| Segment | Size | Pain Point | Willingness to Pay |
|---------|------|------------|-------------------|
| **Career Changers (25-40)** | Large | "I hate my job but don't know what else I can do" | High ($50-200) |
| **University Students (18-24)** | Very Large | "I'm graduating and have no idea what to pursue" | Low ($0-20) |
| **Bootcamp Shoppers** | Medium | "Should I learn coding, data, or design?" | Medium (affiliate value) |

### Secondary Segments (B2B)
- **Coding Bootcamps** → Pay for qualified leads
- **Universities** → License for career services
- **HR/Recruiters** → Candidate assessment tool

## Launch Phases

### Phase 1: Validate (Month 1-2)
**Goal:** 1,000 users, 50 email signups, qualitative feedback

| Action | Channel | Cost |
|--------|---------|------|
| Post on ProductHunt | Free | $0 |
| Share in Reddit r/careerguidance, r/cscareerquestions | Free | $0 |
| LinkedIn personal posts | Free | $0 |
| Twitter/X thread about "psychology of career choice" | Free | $0 |

**Success Metrics:**
- [ ] 1,000 quiz completions
- [ ] 5% email capture rate
- [ ] NPS > 40 from feedback

---

### Phase 2: Optimize (Month 3-4)
**Goal:** 10,000 users, 5% premium conversion, first $1K revenue

| Action | Channel | Cost |
|--------|---------|------|
| Add email gate before results (soft) | Product | $0 |
| Launch premium tier ($29) | Product | Stripe fees |
| SEO content: "What tech career suits me?" | Blog | $0-500 |
| Collect testimonials + success stories | Users | $0 |

**Success Metrics:**
- [ ] 500 emails collected
- [ ] 2-5% premium conversion ($1,500-3,000)
- [ ] 3+ testimonials with photos

---

### Phase 3: Scale (Month 5-6)
**Goal:** 50,000 users, bootcamp partnerships, $10K MRR

| Action | Channel | Cost |
|--------|---------|------|
| Partner with 3-5 bootcamps (affiliate) | Outreach | $0 |
| Facebook/Instagram ads targeting "career change" | Paid | $500-2000/mo |
| YouTube creator partnerships | Influencer | $200-1000 |
| University career center outreach | B2B Sales | $0 |

**Success Metrics:**
- [ ] 3 paying bootcamp partners
- [ ] CAC < $5 for free users
- [ ] $10K+ monthly revenue

## Marketing Channels

### Organic (Free)
1. **SEO Content**
   - "What tech career is right for me quiz"
   - "Frontend vs Backend personality"
   - "Signs you should be a data scientist"

2. **Social Proof**
   - Screenshot results + "This was scary accurate"
   - Before/after career change stories
   - "I took this quiz and 6 months later I'm a UX designer"

3. **Community Seeding**
   - Reddit AMAs in career subreddits
   - Discord servers for career changers
   - LinkedIn thought leadership posts

### Paid (When Profitable)
1. **Meta Ads:** Target "career change," "learn to code," "bootcamp"
2. **Google Ads:** "career aptitude test," "tech career quiz"
3. **YouTube Pre-roll:** 15-sec "Find your perfect tech career in 5 min"

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Low conversion to premium | Test different price points ($19, $29, $49) |
| Bootcamps won't partner | Prove lead quality with free trial |
| Users distrust "quiz" accuracy | Add testimonials, success stories, validation data |
| Competitors copy us | Build email list + community before they can react |
